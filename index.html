Never solved one before? Start here
You do not need to know anything about cubes. Three things happen:
  1. You photograph all six sides. The app works out where every colour is.
  2. You check it got them right. Tap any square that looks wrong and fix it.
  3. You follow the moves. One at a time, with a picture of exactly which part to turn and which way.
Do not turn the cube at all until the app tells you to. Even one twist between now and the end will throw the whole thing off.

What do the letters mean?

Every diagram below shows the cube facing you, the same way you are holding it. The shaded part is the slice that turns, and the arrow is the way it goes. A tick mark after the letter (like R’) means the same slice, opposite way. A 2 means turn it twice.
